Experimental explanatory PCM: structural screening is not global identification or scientific acceptance. Population residuals are iid zero-mean Gaussians, not sample-centered finite-panel effects. Exchangeability conditional on predictors is an assumption. Residual rater SD measures between-rater severity variation, not within-rater consistency or the Patz HRM. Coefficients are associations on unit adjacent logits; they do not establish causal effects. Decomposed means weight unique observed performances equally. They are not latent habitual attributes; deviations are not detrended, and need not sum to zero across rating rows. With one performance per person, the person effect combines stable person and unique performance; those variances are not separated. Shared-performance/local dependence beyond declared effects is not modeled. More ratings do not create new independent performances.
