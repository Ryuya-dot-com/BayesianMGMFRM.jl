Effects/SDs: unit adjacent logits; coefficients: logits per supplied predictor unit (different columns need not be commensurable); white coordinates: standard-normal units. Positive person/response coefficients raise ratings; positive facet coefficients raise severity and lower ratings. Whole-fit numerical qualification: true. explanatory PCM: structural screening is not global identification or scientific acceptance. Population residuals are iid zero-mean Gaussians, not sample-centered finite-panel effects. Exchangeability conditional on predictors is an assumption. Residual rater SD measures between-rater severity variation, not within-rater consistency or the Patz HRM. Coefficients are associations on unit adjacent logits; they do not establish causal effects. With one performance per person, the person effect combines stable person and unique performance; those variances are not separated. Shared-performance/local dependence beyond declared effects is not modeled. More ratings do not create new independent performances. Few owners, sparse overlap and weak category information can leave SDs and coefficients prior-sensitive even when the structural screen passes.
