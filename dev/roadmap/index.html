Development Roadmap

The aim is a reproducible path from a rating question to an explicit model, an adequately precise estimate, interpretable uncertainty and fit, and saved results that can be reopened. Ordinary one-dimensional 1PL/MFRM and generalized scalar models are primary routes; additional dimensions or rater mechanisms need a substantive reason. The value sought is a coherent Julia analysis with explicit assumptions, usable uncertainty, reusable outputs and efficient computation at the precision the question requires.

This page describes the local development version. It does not promise release dates or change the statuses in Scope and Releases. An implemented feature, statistical evidence, supported operations and an installed release are different things. No general speed advantage over other software is established.

Priorities and completion criteria

The first finish is an accepted foundation for the declared existing workflows. The wider planned product also includes the bounded capability and application outcomes above. Completing the foundation does not complete those branches; completing every branch is not a prerequisite for the first foundation delivery.

  1. Prepare the selected foundation for acceptance. The immediate development outcome is one identified source and matching guide with the existing scalar, normalized independent-D2, D1 fixed-slope and mean-profile reference workflows mapped to their actual operation and environment evidence. Preserve the separate eight-result-type saved-output scope. Identify and resolve only missing or affected cells; a new inventory is unnecessary where the mapping already exists. This preparation retains the restricted statistical scope and does not itself promote experimental models.
  2. Obtain independent acceptance and finalize the offered contract. Scientific review must assess the target, evidence and permitted conclusions; an unfamiliar user must reproduce the promised journey and interpret its warnings. Resolve blocking findings and fix the API, scope, compatibility policy and instructions for the accepted candidate. New extensions do not continually enlarge this review target.
  3. Deliver the accepted source and manual. Verify the declared installation, normal startup, analysis, reporting and save/reopen paths in the advertised environments, together with version/migration information and the distributed artifacts. An existing preview or a successful local build is not this exit.
  4. Complete the capability branches through their actual dependencies. Design planning can advance independently of 3D correlation or new facets. An application needs the specific composition it uses, not every extension. Criterion-collapse and RW1 keep distinct model, statistical and operation requirements. A usable prototype can precede statistical acceptance, while a detection or prediction claim cannot precede its evidence. These are dependencies, not a schedule to execute every branch simultaneously. A branch can advance alongside foundation preparation when its own prerequisites are met; foundation delivery is not its entry requirement.

A demonstrated mathematical, data-preservation or required-operation defect takes priority in the affected route. Preserve valid estimates and rerun only missing or invalidated work. Performance work should address a measured cost in the selected operation at useful precision. Numerical convergence cannot repair model misfit, and a faster gradient alone does not establish a faster analysis.

The learning guide, saved comparison workflow and reproduction guide supply existing operations. Scope and Releases defines acceptance conditions. Signed halo/horns, general impressions, exploratory structures and arbitrary combinations remain conditional research directions, not additions to the bounded completion criteria above.

Existing comparison evidence and its limits

The four native ConQuest ordinary/category-use fits are complete. The worked comparison explains their fitted-count identity, inconclusive AIC advantage and different uncertainty treatment. Report the observed discrepancy even when collapse-class support is low; these controls do not establish recovery of the target mechanism or classification performance. Exact native mixture/prior parity remains unverified, not disproved. This completed comparison informs interpretation; it is not the next development milestone or a reason to launch a larger grid.

Selected Chopin posterior summaries have been compared with directly written Stan and brms specifications; a separate observed-group severity comparison uses direct Stan. These are model-specific checks, not general equivalence. brms and direct Stan are two specification routes using the same Stan engine. Numerical agreement does not remove the applications' misfit or sensitivity, and differing timing scopes do not establish a general speed ranking.

The ConQuest binary-Rasch check uses a comparison-specific Julia prior adapter; it does not verify the public PCM prior or criterion-collapse mixture. The separate native category-use comparison uses MML, with fixed global estimates for its conditional predictive calculation. It does not integrate parameter uncertainty in the same way as the saved Julia Bayesian predictions. The study-specific inputs and execution environments are not bundled by this page. For an analysis, use the documented model/backend operations rather than assuming that a general comparison launcher is available.

An identical-posterior check must match likelihood, scale, joint prior and reported quantities. A comparison of competing analyses may use different models, priors or estimators, but must state those differences and preserve a common question. Plug-in fitted values, posterior intervals and predictive tails are not interchangeable; neither are MML information criteria and Bayesian evidence. Model-family overlap, numerical agreement, statistical calibration and practical usefulness are different claims. Apply the same evidence standard to this package and external software.

For claim-specific requirements, see Evidence for Model Extensions. For current operations, use Scope and Releases and Contributing and reproduction.
