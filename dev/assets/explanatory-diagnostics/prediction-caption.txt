Note. Posterior category probabilities for explicitly selected rating rows: means and 95.0% pointwise credible intervals. Diamonds mark structural zeros outside the row's declared support. These are intervals for probabilities, not future-rating predictive intervals. Conditioning: known_owners_and_stored_owner_attributes; these are not new-owner or causal predictor effects. Row attributes and probability MCSE are retained in the report. Whole-fit numerical qualification: true. explanatory PCM: structural screening is not global identification or scientific acceptance. Population residuals are iid zero-mean Gaussians, not sample-centered finite-panel effects. Exchangeability conditional on predictors is an assumption. Residual rater SD measures between-rater severity variation, not within-rater consistency or the Patz HRM. Coefficients are associations on unit adjacent logits; they do not establish causal effects. Decomposed means weight unique observed performances equally. They are not latent habitual attributes; deviations are not detrended, and need not sum to zero across rating rows. With one performance per person, the person effect combines stable person and unique performance; those variances are not separated. Shared-performance/local dependence beyond declared effects is not modeled. More ratings do not create new independent performances. Few owners, sparse overlap and weak category information can leave SDs and coefficients prior-sensitive even when the structural screen passes. Observed rating-pattern issues: 11 constant-score. Counts refer to issue records within steps-owner groups, not distinct people or ratings. Individual IDs, scores and counts remain in report.response_patterns.issues. All observations/categories are retained; these are descriptive warnings, not exclusion or quality verdicts.
