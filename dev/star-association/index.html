Star-Factorized Multi-Criterion Ordinal Association

Unreleased documentation preview — 30 October 2026

This manual includes unreleased interfaces and examples. It is not the documentation of an installed release. Model support and scientific acceptance remain as stated on each page.

When raters evaluate each performance or entity across $D \ge 2$ criteria that may have different numbers of score categories ($K_c \ge 2$, including mixed ordinal and binary criteria), BayesianMGMFRM.Experimental.StarAssociation (or BayesianMGMFRM.Experimental.joint_rating_spec(...; association = :star)) estimates:

  1. Correlated multidimensional entity abilities and rater severities under zero-sum orthonormal Helmert bases and rowwise LKJCholesky correlation priors with log-normal marginal scale priors.
  2. Criterion-specific partial-credit thresholds (K_c - 1 sum-to-zero steps per criterion) and explicit criterion intercepts.
  3. Within-event residual star association ($\phi_{0c}$) around a declared root criterion ($c = 0$), reducing exact event-table normalization from $\prod_{c=0}^{D-1} K_c$ cells to $K_0 \bigl(1 + \sum_{c=1}^{D-1} K_c\bigr)$ operations per rating event while retaining exact analytical gradients.
  4. Optional unit-Jacobian reference-profile prior transport on the root criterion margin when reference_prior = true.

Parameterization, numerical defaults and their limits

Unbalanced multi-facet rating designs often combine heavily observed panel raters or entities with sparsely observed ones. By default:

  • ParameterizationConfig() orders facet levels by descending observation count (basis_order = :descending_schur) and uses a dimensionless working-information rule (entity_mode = :cp_ncp, rater_mode = :cp_ncp, cutoff 8.0) to choose centered or standardized noncentered contrast coordinates.
  • Fitting uses independent dispersed initialization (init = :overdispersed), Stan-style three-stage windowed adaptation (adaptation = :stan) and a diagonal reference approximation based on design counts and prior scales (initial_metric = :prior_fisher). The metric does not use observed scores.

These choices are motivated by centered/noncentered theory and bounded engineering checks. They are not a proof of optimal NUTS mixing or scientific prior defaults. In particular, Papaspiliopoulos, Roberts and Sköld (2007) explain complementary parametrizations and partial noncentering; they do not derive the package's PCM/NUTS cutoff of eight. Select priors for the application and inspect both whole-fit diagnostics and focal mean/interval Monte Carlo error.

Exact identities and working approximations

For a single Gaussian working facet with positive count weights $W=\operatorname{diag}(c)$, total $N=\sum c_i$ and an orthonormal sum-zero basis $H$, the Schur identity is

\[\{H^\top(W-cc^\top/N)H\}^{-1}=H^\top W^{-1}H.\]

The reciprocal diagonal defines the effective counts used here. Sorted counts make these values invariant to relabeling and isolate the most observed levels in early contrasts. This identity does not give the full crossed ordinal model's marginal posterior curvature: the other facet, thresholds, scales, correlations and within-event association also matter.

At a reference PCM profile, the exact natural-parameter information is the score variance. With maximum canonical score $m=K-1$, the implementation uses

\[\widetilde{\mathcal I} =\min\{\max[\operatorname{Var}_{p^{(0)}}(Y),10^{-6}],m(m+2)/12\}, \qquad \kappa_j=e^{2\mu_{\log\sigma}}\min_{c}\widetilde{\mathcal I}_{c}\,n_{\mathrm{eff},j}.\]

Binary support uses 0.25. The upper bound is the uniform-profile variance, not the largest possible variance on the support. Negative step spacing can activate that cap; concentrated profiles can activate the floor. The scale is the squared prior median SD, not the prior mean variance. Thus this is a routing proxy, not exact posterior information. Contrasts with $\kappa_j\ge8$ use CP; others use NCP. The cutoff, clipping and diagonal initial metric remain disclosed engineering choices. They retain their bounded stress-test support; they do not establish efficiency for all sparse designs, scales or associations.

The exact target density, CP/NCP transformations and Jacobians are separate from this routing choice. Changing coordinates must preserve the physical likelihood and joint prior. The Glass-Box functions below expose those identities. Choosing a different coordinate partition is not a change to the scientific model.

Step-size search and failures

For adaptation=:stan, a finite positive step size returned by the initial search is used without an extra package floor/cap. A nonfinite/nonpositive search result raises an error; it is not replaced by a seemingly successful fixed step. Inspect the initial state, prior scales and metric before retrying. An explicit step_size is validated and used as requested. The opt-in adaptation=:clamped_regularized still applies its declared bounds and records a warning. Removing a silent fallback does not establish posterior qualification; the normal diagnostic and focal-precision requirements remain.

What qualification covers

qualify(fitted; max_rhat=1.01, min_ess=400.0, min_bfmi=0.3) checks all raw and transformed coordinates and every retained sampler record. The original names rhat_max, ess_min, and ebfmi_min remain aliases; do not pass both spellings. A partial check with check_transformed=false reports the omitted gate as missing and cannot return whole-fit or overall qualification.

The additional focal gates apply only to result.focal_parameters: the finite entity correlations cor_entity_direct[branch], pairing each branch with the root. The default MCSE limits are 0.01 for the mean and 0.02 for the 2.5%/97.5% interval endpoints. They do not qualify all population correlations, residual associations, contrasts, ranks or predicted event probabilities. Those require their own reported MCSE and a justified precision criterion. Changing the graph root also changes this focal list. Passing these numerical gates does not establish prior adequacy, fit, identification or scientific validity; source warnings remain.

Unified Practitioner Workflow Example

The bundled example supplies 36 artificial ratings (four entities, three raters, three criteria) and all nine required prior settings. These values demonstrate the interface; they are not a sample-size recommendation or scientific defaults. Choose and assess priors for your application before substantive analysis.

using BayesianMGMFRM
include(joinpath(pkgdir(BayesianMGMFRM), "examples", "star_association.jl"))
prepared = StarAssociationExample.inputs()  # No fitting or simulated ratings.
spec = prepared.spec
summary_info = design_summary(spec)

For your own data, each row needs observation_id, rating_event_id, entity, rater, criterion, and score. Each complete event contains one row per criterion. Criterion specifications use criterion and levels, with one role = :root. Inspect prepared.rows, prepared.criterion_specs and prepared.prior for a complete example. Name the specifications criterion_specs so they do not shadow the criteria function.

The following operations generate prior values or ratings; keep their seed:

q_raw = prior_draw(spec; seed = 42)
synthetic = simulate(spec; seed = 42)

This block runs four chains. If you already have a saved fit from the matching source and environment, use the reopening block instead. Short artificial examples may fail precision gates; preserve those warnings rather than treating completion of sampling as numerical qualification.

result = fit(spec; ndraws = 500, warmup = 500, chains = 4, seed = 20261030)
out = mktempdir(mkpath("results/star-analysis"); cleanup = false)
fit_path = joinpath(out, "star-fit.jls")
save_fit(fit_path, result)
answer = StarAssociationExample.result(result)
answer.gate.result.numerically_qualified
answer.posterior
answer.difference
save_report(joinpath(out, "star-summary.json"), answer.posterior)

The helper also returns entity summaries, finite-cohort reliability, ranks, classification probabilities, and event-level WAIC/PSIS-LOO. Inspect the qualification and criterion warnings before interpreting them. Numerical qualification does not establish prior adequacy or model fit.

Use prior_sensitivity(result, alternative_prior) or a vector of named prior alternatives to inspect focal changes while preserving physical coordinates. Each alternative must specify a complete prior, as required by PriorConfig. An exactly identical resolved prior returns status = :unchanged_prior: the baseline empirical distribution is reused and mean shifts are exactly zero. pareto_k, r_eff and psis_n_eff are nothing (JSON null) because no importance-sampling adjustment is needed. The existing alt_psis_* field names are retained; their quantiles use the weighted empirical CDF. This status does not certify convergence or prior adequacy, and does not erase sampling warnings or posterior uncertainty. Focal MCMC MCSE is not computed by this sensitivity report. Constant weights under a different prior do not receive this exemption. Saved reports retain their original contents; native fits must still be opened with their producer source and environment.

To compare two fitted Star models, use the shared entry point:

comparison = compare_models("association" => result, "no_residual_association" => no_residual_result)
comparison.primary_name
comparison.baseline_name
comparison.waic_diff
comparison.psis_loo_diff
comparison.any_sampling_warning

no_residual_result must be an already fitted alternative on the same observed events. Event order is aligned by ID; scores, actual entity/rater labels, criterion IDs, category supports and scoring directions must match. Different priors, roots and residual association terms remain valid model differences. With include_phi=false, residual association is fixed to zero while entity/rater correlations remain estimated. Both criteria use complete events, not individual criterion ratings. The first model is the primary model; differences are primary minus baseline without an automatic ranking. Current in-memory criteria(fit) results can be compared without recalculating the likelihood. Older records without observed-event identity are refused for comparison, while their saved reports remain readable.

SEs retain the existing entity + rater - observation variance approximation and observation-variance floor. The output identifies clipping and repeated entity/rater pairs; the latter lack an intersection-cluster adjustment. These SEs are not MCMC MCSE or a general calibration guarantee. See the comparison and prediction-unit guide.

Reopen the saved analysis without refitting:

reloaded = load_fit(fit_path)
reopened_answer = StarAssociationExample.result(reloaded)

Optional figures require using CairoMakie and are called with BayesianMGMFRM.plot(result; kind = :posterior), :diagnostics, or :predictive. The predictive figure generates replicated category counts from all retained draws, conditional on the fitted entities and raters. It displays observed and expected proportions with 95% replicated intervals, separately by criterion; these are predictive intervals, not credible intervals for the expected rates. Use seed to reproduce this check. Figure notes are printed outside the image.

Glass-Box Researcher Inspection

Methodological researchers can inspect exact basis partitions, coordinate diffeomorphisms, triangular change-of-variables Jacobians, analytical gradients, and Stan data dictionaries directly:

ebasis = facet_basis(spec, :entity)
coords = coordinates(spec, q_raw)
q_ncp = to_ncp_reference(spec, q_raw)
log_jac = log_jacobian_to_ncp(spec, q_raw)
lp, grad = logdensity_and_gradient(spec, q_raw)
sdata = stan_data(spec)

BayesianMGMFRM.Experimental.StarAssociation — Module
StarAssociation

Multidimensional rating-event ordinal association model (D >= 2 criteria with arbitrary mixed category supports K_c >= 2) using a star-factorized conditional potential around a declared root criterion, correlated zero-sum entity and rater effects (LKJCholesky), and optional reference-profile marginal prior transport.

    • Specification & Design Audit: contract, model, model_from_events, model_from_indexed, with_options, validate_design, design_summary, parameter_names
    • Prior & Synthetic Simulation: prior_draw, simulate (alias: generate), prior_predictive_check
    • MCMC Estimation & Multi-Worker Checkpointing: fit, prepare_fit, run_chain_worker, worker_status, collect_fit, from_saved_blocks
    • Qualification, Summaries, Contrasts & Measurement Decisions: qualify (alias: diagnostics), posterior_summary (alias: report), facet_summary, category_summary, contrast, event_probabilities, reliability, ranking, classification
    • Streaming Fit Criteria, Predictive Checks, Comparison & Sensitivity: waic, psis_loo, criteria, predictive_check (alias: ppc), compare_models (alias: compare), evaluate, prior_sensitivity
    • Persistence: save_fit / load_fit (aliases: save_result / load_result), save_report / load_report

  2. Glass-Box Researcher Transparency Surface

    • Basis & Coordinate Geometry: facet_basis, coordinates, to_ncp_reference, from_ncp_reference, reparameterize_point, reparameterize_draws, log_jacobian_to_ncp

BayesianMGMFRM.Experimental.StarAssociation.FacetBasis — Type
FacetBasis

Precomputed zero-sum orthonormal Helmert basis mapping and Schur-complement effective contrast counts for one facet (:entity or :rater).

Under basis_order = :descending_schur (default):

  • Levels are ordered by non-increasing observation count (perm), ensuring that contrasts 1:(k-1) span exclusively the k most-observed levels and put exact weight 0 on sparser levels.
  • effective_counts[j] equals the exact Rao-Searle Schur-complement contrast precision [(H' * W^{-1} * H)_{jj}]^{-1} = j*(j+1) / (sum_{m=1}^j c_{(m)}^{-1} + j^2 * c_{(j+1)}^{-1}) in a Gaussian working design with diagonal count weights W, after profiling out its intercept and all other n - 2 contrasts. It is not the marginal posterior precision of the full crossed ordinal model. This quantity depends solely on the sorted count multiset (100% invariant to all n! permutations of user level IDs, including ties) and satisfies c_{(j+1)} <= eff[j] < 2 * c_{(j+1)}.

BayesianMGMFRM.Experimental.StarAssociation.fit — Function
fit(m::StarModel; ndraws, warmup, chains=4, seed=20261030, checkpoint_dir=nothing, kwargs...)

Sample the joint posterior of m via AdvancedHMC NUTS.

Default sampler configuration is 100% literature-grounded:

  • init = :overdispersed (init_radius = 2.0): independent per-chain overdispersed draws free of outcome data snooping (Gelman & Rubin, 1992; Vehtari et al., 2021).
  • adaptation = :stan: unmodified AdvancedHMC.StanHMCAdaptor (Stan 3-stage Welford diagonal mass adaptation + Hoffman & Gelman, 2014 find_good_stepsize and unclamped Nesterov dual averaging).
  • initial_metric = :prior_fisher: outcome-free Schur-complement design/prior scale preconditioning during initial fast window 1 (or :unit for identity).

Optional strategies (init = :prior | :stan_uniform | :multipath_lbfgs | :shared_warmstart, adaptation = :clamped_regularized) are available via explicit keywords for benchmarking and ablation.
