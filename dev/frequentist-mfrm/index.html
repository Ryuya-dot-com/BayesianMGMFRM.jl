Frequentist 1D JMLE and MMLE

When you want fast point calibration, classical FACETS/ConQuest/mfrmr/mfrmpy tables, or a direct frequentist baseline alongside a Bayesian posterior on the exact same rating specification, pass method = :jmle or method = :mmle to fit.

Non-Bayesian estimation is intentionally scoped to one-dimensional unit-slope Rating Scale (RSM) and Partial Credit (PCM) models across both standard three-facet FacetSpec (person, rater, item) and general user-named multi-facet mfrm_spec designs. Multidimensional, free-slope (GPCM), predictor-regression, and random-interaction models remain Bayesian routes.

Quick start: 3-facet and general multi-facet specifications

For a three-facet table (person, rater, item), reuse FacetData and mfrm_spec directly:

using BayesianMGMFRM

data = FacetData(ratings; person=:person, rater=:rater, item=:item,
    score=:score, category_levels=0:4)
spec = mfrm_spec(data; thresholds=:rating_scale) # or :partial_credit

fit_jmle = fit(spec; method=:jmle)
fit_mmle = fit(spec; method=:mmle, quad_points=31, population_sd=1.0)

For a general $F$-facet table (for example Person × Rater × Task × Criterion), mfrm_spec can be called without prior_sd when only frequentist estimation is needed, or with prior_sd when you want to run both frequentist and Bayesian fits on the identical specification object:

spec_4f = mfrm_spec(writing;
    person=:Person,
    facets=(Rater=:Rater, Task=:Task, Criterion=:Criterion),
    score=:Score,
    steps=:global,          # or steps=:Criterion for PCM
    category_levels=0:5,
)

jml_rsm = fit(spec_4f; method=:jmle)
mml_rsm = fit(spec_4f; method=:mmle, quad_points=31, population_sd=1.0)

Alignment with FACETS, ConQuest, mfrmr, and mfrmpy

All conventions are explicit so results match across packages when the same estimator and constraints are selected:

  1. Additive logit orientation: By default, person ability enters with +1 and every non-person facet enters with -1 (difficulty / severity):

    \[\log\frac{P(Y_n = k)}{P(Y_n = k - 1)} = \theta_{p(n)} - \sum_{f=1}^{F} \beta_{f, j_f(n)} - \tau_{g(n), k}.\]

    Pass positive_facets = [:Rater] if a facet uses easiness/leniency orientation.
  2. Identification gauge (centering and noncenter_facet):
    • centering = :sum_to_zero (default) constrains free levels of each centered facet to sum to zero ($\sum_\ell \beta_{f, \ell} = 0$) and step thresholds within each step owner to sum to zero ($\sum_s \tau_{g, s} = 0$), matching mfrmr, mfrmpy, FACETS, and ConQuest (lconstraints=items).
    • centering = :reference_first fixes the first level of each centered facet to zero, matching the default gauge of BayesianMGMFRM's FacetDesign.
    • In JMLE, noncenter_facet = :person (default) leaves interior person abilities unconstrained while centering non-person facets. Individual hard anchors (anchors = (rater = Dict("R1" => 0.0), ...)) fix the anchored levels directly.
  3. MMLE population distribution (population_sd, estimate_sd, estimate_mean):
    • estimate_sd = false, estimate_mean = false (default, population_sd = 1.0): integrates $\theta_p \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_0^2)$ over Golub–Welsch Gauss–Hermite quadrature (quadrature = :gauss_hermite, quad_points = 31 default, or quadrature = :rectangular), matching fixed-SD MML in mfrmr and mfrmpy.
    • estimate_sd = true, estimate_mean = false: estimates $\theta_p \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ jointly with sum-to-zero non-person facets, matching ConQuest set lconstraints=items; estimate ! method=gauss.
    • estimate_sd = true, estimate_mean = true: estimates both mean and SD $\theta_p \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$ with sum-to-zero non-person facets, matching mfrmr population_formula = ~ 1.
  4. Extreme-score persons in JMLE (extreme_adjustment):
    • extreme_adjustment = 0.0 (default): exact unadjusted JMLE. Persons with all-minimum or all-maximum scores have no finite unpenalized MLE ($\mp\infty$), contribute zero gradient at the limit, and are reported with finite_mle = false and estimate = missing alongside finite Warm WLE scores (wle, wle_se).
    • extreme_adjustment = 0.3: applies the FACETS EXTRIM = 0.3 score adjustment to extreme person totals.

Standard errors, person scores, and fit statistics

Frequentist uncertainty quantities are not Bayesian posterior credible intervals or MCMC Monte Carlo standard errors. Reports keep three uncertainty quantities distinct:

  • se (:constrained_observed_information): Exact delta-method standard error from the inverse observed Fisher information on the identified free coordinates (J * inv(H_free) * J'), computed via exact block Schur complement in unadjusted JMLE and exact marginal-log-likelihood Hessian in MMLE.
  • conditional_se (ModelSE in JMLE): FACETS-style diagonal conditional approximation 1 / sqrt(sum(w_r * Var(Y_r | eta_hat_r))), holding all other parameters fixed.
  • model_se & real_se (RealSE): model_se equals se in MMLE and conditional_se in JMLE (matching mfrmr and mfrmpy), with misfit-inflated real_se = model_se * sqrt(max(1.0, infit)).

Person scores (facet_summary(fit; facet = :person) or fit_report(fit).person_scores) provide:

  • In MMLE: eap, eap_sd (quadrature posterior SD conditional on point calibration; calibration uncertainty is not propagated), eap_q05/eap_q50/eap_q95, Warm's bias-reduced wle and wle_se, and fixed-calibration mle / mle_se.
  • In JMLE: joint estimate, joint Hessian se, conditional_se, and fixed-calibration wle / wle_se.

Fit diagnostics (fit_stats(fit), facets_report(fit)) report Infit and Outfit mean squares together with Wright & Masters (1982, Eq. 4.27) FACETS degrees of freedom and Wilson–Hilferty cube-root ZSTD (df >= 1 safeguard and optional zstd_cap = 9.0), alongside homogeneous-variance infit_zstd_engine and outfit_zstd_engine columns and both composite (ptmea) and person-only (ptmea_person) point-measure correlations.

Inspecting, comparing, saving, and plotting

qualify(mml_rsm)                        # gradient, positive-definite Hessian & boundary check
rep = fit_report(mml_rsm)               # portable summary, facet/step/person tables, reliability
contrast(mml_rsm; facet=:Rater, left="R1", right="R4")

# Compare RSM vs. PCM under MMLE (AIC / BIC / likelihood-ratio summary):
spec_pcm = mfrm_spec(writing; person=:Person,
    facets=(Rater=:Rater, Task=:Task, Criterion=:Criterion),
    score=:Score, steps=:Criterion, category_levels=0:5)
mml_pcm = fit(spec_pcm; method=:mmle, quad_points=31)
cmp = compare_models("RSM" => mml_rsm, "PCM" => mml_pcm; criterion=:aic)

save_fit("mml-rsm.jls", mml_rsm)
save_report("mml-rsm.json", rep)

using CairoMakie
BayesianMGMFRM.plot(mml_rsm; kind=:posterior)  # point estimates + 95% asymptotic Wald CIs
BayesianMGMFRM.plot(mml_rsm; kind=:wright)     # multi-facet Wright map
BayesianMGMFRM.plot(mml_rsm; kind=:pathway)    # Infit MNSQ vs. logit Pathway map
BayesianMGMFRM.plot(cmp; kind=:comparison)     # MMLE ΔAIC / ΔBIC comparison

Bayesian-only MCMC operations (posterior_summary, posterior_mcse, waic, psis_loo, prior_sensitivity) and JMLE AIC/BIC comparisons (invalid under Neyman–Scott incidental person parameters) are deterministically refused with guidance to the appropriate frequentist or Bayesian verb.
