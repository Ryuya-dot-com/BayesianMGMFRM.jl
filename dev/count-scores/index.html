Analyze correct counts with known totals

Use this route when each response reports correct words out of a known number of scored words. Keep both integers: 4/5 and 40/50 have the same proportion but supply different information under a binomial model. Do not convert rounded percentages into invented counts.

Input and model

Each table row is one response, with correct, total, speaker, recording and listener IDs. Counts must satisfy 0 <= correct <= total and total > 0. Missing values and fractional counts refuse. An unadministered transcription is missing by design, not zero correct. Reconcile the denominator with the actual scoring rule before selecting observed rows.

using BayesianMGMFRM
using BayesianMGMFRM.Legacy
using Distributions: Normal, LogNormal

spec = mfrm_spec(responses;
    person=:speaker, score=:correct, performance=:recording,
    facets=(recording_effect=:recording, listener_effect=:listener),
    observation=(family=:beta_binomial, trials=:total),
    observation_prior=(precision=LogNormal(log(8), .6),),
    population=(person=LogNormal(log(.5), .35),
        recording_effect=.3, listener_effect=LogNormal(log(.4), .35)),
    predictors=(response=(rate=Normal(0, .6),),),
    intercept=Normal(0, 1))

validate_design(spec)
prior = prior_predictive_check(spec; seed=101, ndraws=100)

These priors illustrate syntax; they are not universal recommendations. Each declared owner needs a population residual SD, fixed numerically or assigned a LogNormal prior. The mean uses the same person/facet/response predictor rules as explanatory ratings, including unique-recording within/between decomposition. A positive listener-facet effect lowers success probability; call it a recognition/reproduction tendency when that describes the task, rather than assuming it is a rater's severity. No 1.7 multiplier is applied. trials is reserved as a diagnostic grouping name in this count route.

For family=:binomial, omit observation_prior. Conditional on the declared effects, scored words are modeled as independent trials with one probability. The beta-binomial allows an exchangeable response-specific success probability: its beta distribution has mean p and precision phi. Its count variance is n*p*(1-p)*(1+(n-1)/(1+phi)). Smaller precision means greater overdispersion; 1/(1+phi) describes within-response dependence, not rater reliability. Neither model reconstructs word difficulty or word order from a total count.

With one trial, both likelihoods are exactly Bernoulli. If every row has one trial, precision is informed only by its prior; the design report warns about this. Between-speaker, recording and within-response variation are different sources. One recording per speaker cannot identify two unrestricted owner variances. This scalar route does not add random slopes or multiple outcomes.

Fit, check and visualize

result = fit(spec; chains=4, warmup=800, ndraws=2000, seed=102,
    chain_execution=:threads, max_parallel_chains=4)
qualify(result)
report = fit_report(result;
    parameters=["coefficient[response,rate]", "precision"], include_draws=true)
ppc = predictive_check(result; seed=103, ndraws=300)
prediction = predict(result; rows=[1, 2])

using CairoMakie
BayesianMGMFRM.plot(prior; kind=:predictive)
BayesianMGMFRM.plot(report; kind=:diagnostics)
BayesianMGMFRM.plot(ppc; kind=:predictive)
BayesianMGMFRM.plot(prediction; kind=:prediction)
save_fit("count-fit.jls", result)
save_report("count-prediction.json", prediction)
BayesianMGMFRM.plot(load_report("count-prediction.json"); kind=:prediction)

Iteration counts are examples. Check whole-fit diagnostics and focal MCSE, including interval endpoints; a passing numerical check is not adequate fit. For a binomial report, omit precision from the requested parameter names. PPC defaults to separate trial counts and compares zero/perfect rates, response proportions and their variation. groupby=:person or a declared facet instead inspects an owner. Equal-response and pooled word proportions differ when denominators vary; neither weighting is automatically the research estimand. PPC intervals are pointwise, and their endpoint MCSE is not assessed.

predict reports expected correct counts/proportions and zero/perfect probabilities at the supplied trial counts. Its CrIs represent parameter uncertainty, not future individual counts. New rows require known owners and stored owner attributes; they must declare total. Changing the denominator predicts under the same probability model; it does not establish invariance to sentence difficulty.

Use prior_sensitivity for declared coefficient/SD factors and beta-binomial precision, keeping Jacobians unpowered. Review weight and focal-MCSE warnings. criteria(result; unit=:rating) and compare_models(...; criterion=:psis_loo) compare count probabilities for identical counts, denominators and known owners. They do not score a new speaker/recording/listener or supply a population sampling SE. Neither collapsed-category scores nor continuous densities share this measure. Retain the receipt from save_report(...; return_receipt=true) and supply its SHA-256 to reopen sensitivity/comparison reports with load_report.

The normalized observation models follow the Stan probability functions. Beta-binomial mean/precision also corresponds to the brms mu/phi parameterization. Matching a likelihood does not establish matching owner priors, identification, full-model coverage or posterior results across software.
